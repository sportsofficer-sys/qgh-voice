### DF Profile with Unserviceable Microphone

Note: Aircraft with unserviceable microphone when pressing the PTT generates a tone only uses the following procedure to request information from ground station or give information:

| PTT signal | Meaning in the excerpt |
|---|---|
| ONE CLICK (pressing PTT once) | Yes or affirmative |
| TWO CLICKS | No or negative |
| THREE CLICKS | Say again |
| FOUR CLICKS | Request homing |

### Request for homing and controlled descent

Aircraft request: FOUR CLICKS.

Tower: SPEECHLESS AIRCRAFT (call sign of replying station) HOMING (number) CONFIRM CALLING (call sign of replying station).

Aircraft: ONE CLICK.

Tower: HOMING (number) CONFIRM WISH TO CARRY CONTROLLED DESCENT.

Transcription note: OUT is not visible after CARRY and has not been inserted.

Aircraft: ONE CLICK.

Tower: HOMING (number) CLEARED FOR CONTROLLED DESCENT.

### Confirming additional emergency

CONFIRM ANY OTHER EMERGENCY.

Aircraft reply: ONE CLICK (or) TWO CLICKS.

Note: One click by aircraft indicates additional emergencies in addition to unserviceable microphone.

### Ascertaining additional emergency

(a) CONFIRM ENGINE.

(b) CONFIRM COMPASS.

(c) CONFIRM HYDRAULIC.

(d) CONFIRM INSTRUMENTS.

(e) CONFIRM FUEL.

(f) CONFIRM OXYGEN.

Note: Aircraft confirms or denies additional emergency by one or two clicks respectively.

## Page 99 — Unserviceable microphone continuation

### Ascertaining level

CLIMB OR DESCENT TO NEAREST HALF KILOMETRE (or) THOUSAND FEET. GIVE A LONG TRANSMISSION FOR EVERY KILOMETRE (or) FIVE THOUSAND FEET AND SHORT TRANSMISSION FOR EVERY HALF KILOMETRE (or) ONE THOUSAND FEET.

Transcription note: DESCENT and the listed unit alternatives are retained without correction or interpretation.

### Ascertaining information

(a) GIVE LONG TRANSMISSION PASSING (or) STEADY HEADING (number).

(b) GIVE LONG TRANSMISSION PASSING CHECK ALTITUDE.

(c) GIVE LONG TRANSMISSION AT MDA.

(d) GIVE LONG TRANSMISSION ON AERODROME VISUAL.
