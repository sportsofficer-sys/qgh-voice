# v4.4.3 — Voice Safety & U/S Turns

Status: local browser candidate. Not published.

## Changes

- U/S Compass LEFT NOW and RIGHT NOW can directly reverse an active timed turn without a heading or position jump. STOP TURN NOW remains available to level the aircraft on its current heading.
- Normal and U/S voice routes use the visible control availability before mutating any input. A rejected CONTINUE call no longer changes its heading field.
- Urgent manoeuvre and clock-stop calls cancel a pending restart confirmation and execute through the existing controls.
- The voice parser rejects negated, conditional, delayed, wrong-unit and chained calls instead of executing a partial fragment.
- Addressed QGH information calls such as `RAVEN 21 HOMING 230 CONTINUE` stay radio exchanges; they cannot be mistaken for orbit instructions. U/S Compass grammar also no longer biases recognition toward hidden heading reports.
- Formation leader and stop-following phrases take precedence over generic aircraft selection.
- A report-heading-passing call arms an independent, one-shot measured crossing report. The visual and pilot feedback read `HEADING PASSING nnn°M` (or `HEADING PASSED nnn°M` if delayed).
- Ordinary heading and range pilot reports resample at their actual pilot-transmission time if a controller transmission delayed them.
